P18 order updates

Choose the format your P18 sends. Full order is the default. Additions and voids require a matching order number and terminal. Unmatched changes are shown for review. P18 must send a kitchen void print for automatic voiding.

Share stations on this Wi-Fi

Enter the other tablets' addresses from their green bars. Use a private restaurant Wi-Fi network. Each tablet keeps receiving its own P18 prints if another tablet goes offline. Expo needs the prep tablets connected to show their progress.

Online delivery
